## Reading discipline

This is a **curated comparative reference**, not a proof that all 54 coordinates exhaust reality. It draws on the supplied controlling grammar, the developed Characteristics profiles, the Natural Atlas, and the cumulative Worlds Master. The mathematical examples are concise restatements of standard mathematical constructions, not claims that mathematical theory has verified the grammar. Entries from art, stories, law-like social settings and perception describe completions in a specific practice, interpretation or observer, not necessarily in the physical carrier itself.

For each cell the **irreducible characteristic** is kept distinct from the canonical name. The examples are *candidate completion-events*, not just objects. The criterion is always: **what changed, what made it sufficient, and how does it remain?** For Release, a previously governing constraint must demonstrably lose force; for Repair, a specified misfit must be corrected; for Settlement, a live demand must be discharged. If that condition is missing, the example is only suggestive.

The order of examples favours a concrete natural event, a precise mathematical case, an audible musical case, then diverse perceptual, visual, narrative, architectural, social and computational carriers. “Best” means structurally revealing and discriminable, not experimentally certified. Earlier, less secure cases are retained with a warning rather than quietly changed.
