One sentence. Four readings. One governing achievement.

The challenge is not to find a statement that is true about the sentence. It is to recognise what makes this sentence one completed intelligible whole.

How to play: Read the original sentence, then choose its Operative Centre. The three alternatives are plausible but insufficient or mistaken readings. After you choose, the recorded Centre and its diagnostic are revealed. No partial credit: the craft is exact recognition.
