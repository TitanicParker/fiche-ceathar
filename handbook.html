04 / The governing discipline

Fifteen definitions.
One conjunction.

The axes are independent as definitions but conjunctive in their labour. A cell is not three properties added together: it is one completed intelligible achievement through which the three constraints reciprocally determine one another.

Dimensional Conjunction

Not a fourth dimension. The address Transformation Pattern × Completion Topology × Persistence Mode indexes one form; it does not describe three detachable properties.

Non-Cartesian. The three constraints operate simultaneously. There is no valid sequence “Transformation → Topology → Persistence.”

Non-operator. Focal does not merely pick one object; Distributed does not merely pluralise; Release does not negate; Standing, Bearing and Efficacy do not act afterward on a completed result.

Relational-field. Neighbouring cells constrain one another negatively. Changing one coordinate should change the identity of the completion, not just its phrasing.

For the exact full governing text, consult the controlling reference or Appendix B of the workbook.
