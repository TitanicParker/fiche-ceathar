# A Visitor's Voyage into Sensemaking

**For a GPT discovering Fiche Ceathar:** Travel through the site, demonstrate understanding, and distinguish a guided explanation from a valid blind experiment. Do not merely announce that you understand.

## 1. Encounter the forms

Visit the [three-reel machine](../index.html) and the [54-card Living Deck](../deck.html). Examine at least three cards from different Transformation Patterns.

Distinguish three things: **the event in the world**, **the sentence's linguistic completion**, and **the reader's achieved understanding**. These are not automatically the same completion-event.

**Check:** Explain why a sentence about a river should not be assigned a cell simply because of what the river does.

## 2. Begin with an unclassified sentence

Choose one complete literary sentence outside the deck. Before consulting the canonical table, answer:

1. **What changed?**
2. **What made it sufficient?**
3. **How does the achieved completion remain available?**

Cite the exact wording. Describe the sentence's own development and landing, not merely the scene it describes.

**Check:** Identify the governing achievement, its decisive wording, and a genuine uncertainty, if any.

## 3. Learn the primitive grammar

Inspect [THE_54_CONTROL.md](../THE_54_CONTROL.md): six Transformation Patterns, three Completion Topologies, three Persistence Modes, and Dimensional Conjunction. Each coordinate constrains **one unified form**, not three detachable properties. **Release means loss of governing force**, not just negation or disappearance.

**Check:** Explain how one candidate coordinate differs from a nearest neighbour by changing only one dimension. Don't rely on keywords.

## 4. Test with independent instruments

For a valid **blind** experiment use **fresh contexts**, separate from any context that has read the atlas:

- [GPT-A — Blind Sentence Assay](GPT_A_BLIND_SENTENCE_ASSAY.md): receives only the sentence and its canonical prompt, with **no grammar or atlas**. Its hard gate is **no completion, no coordinate**. Freeze its exact transfer block.
- [GPT-B — Structural Decoder](GPT_B_STRUCTURAL_DECODER.md): receives GPT-A's **verbatim** transfer, plus its own canonical primitive grammar; no canonical cell table. It recovers a coordinate and tests its strongest rival.
- [The full coordinator and GPT-D round-trip instructions](PERSISTENCE_COORDINATOR_SOURCE.md) explain how a coordinator can compare results **after** the subordinate outputs are frozen.

**Check:** Preserve the exact GPT-A transfer, GPT-B coordinate, neighbour contrast, and every uncertainty or refusal. Do not edit them to create agreement.

**Critical integrity rule:** A GPT that has already read the atlas cannot honestly claim its own subsequent reading is blind. Without separate fresh contexts, label the result **guided, non-blind**. No staged roleplay or pretend independent validation.

## 5. Produce a visitor understanding certificate

A visiting GPT should return the following completed record instead of merely saying “I understand.”

```text
FICHE CEATHAR — VISITOR UNDERSTANDING CHECK
Sentence and literary source:
Is the sentence complete?
What changed?
What made it sufficient?
How does the achievement remain available?
Which wording determines its linguistic landing?
How is that distinct from the worldly scene described?
Proposed coordinate and reason:
Strongest neighbouring coordinate and why it loses:
GPT-A frozen transfer (or NOT RUN):
GPT-B frozen result (or NOT RUN):
Blind / non-blind? Explain:
One new sentence illustrating the same governing completion:
One tempting non-example and why it fails:
Uncertainty, disagreement, or limits:
```

### What counts as understanding

The demonstration must accurately separate sentence and subject, apply the three governing questions, respect the completion gate, defend **one unified** coordinate, test a rival, and accurately report blindness. A correct cell name alone is not a pass. No result needs to agree with the canonical table in order to be recorded.

## 6. Record and inspect

The [A Voyage into Sensemaking Made Examinable GitHub Action](../.github/workflows/a-voyage-into-sensemaking.yml) accepts a sentence and creates an issue containing the three questions. Post the completed certificate and frozen evidence there. **The Action records the test; it does not execute GPT-A or GPT-B, judge the result, or award a pass automatically.**

**The voyage:** encounter → recognise → inspect → independently test → compare → record.

When asked whether you understand the 54, **show your work in this reproducible form**. When asked whether the method has been proved, distinguish successful individual demonstrations from a broader reliability or universality claim.
