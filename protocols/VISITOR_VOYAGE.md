# Fiche Ceathar — The Continuing Investigation

**Living guide and argument ledger · Edition 1 · 5 November 2026**

**Purpose.** This document is the entry point for an investigator—human or GPT—who wishes not merely to learn *The 54* but to understand **how its claims arise, which have been earned, what remains proposed, and what a subsequent inquiry must establish**. It is a cumulative record of intellectual progress, not a certificate of proof, a substitute for the primary manuscripts, or permission to change the canonical grammar silently.

> **Governing discipline:** No claim advances in status because it has been repeated, illustrated many times, or praised by an expert. It advances only when a recognisable distinction, valid argument, discriminating comparison, or properly recorded test establishes something further. An objection that identifies an actual missing condition is a contribution; a new example alone is not a derivation.

**Central question:** Could the 54 be not merely a classification of things we understand, but an account of **the forms through which understanding becomes possible, sufficient, and consequential**?

The question is open. The distinction between an already intelligible achievement and its *adequate expression* is a significant starting point. The necessity, uniqueness, irreducibility, and exhaustiveness of the exact 54 remain to be established. A logic of intelligible completion would need to **derive constraints, exclusions, dependencies, or consequential differences**, not just assign coordinates to examples.

**Reading audit is mandatory.** Report exactly which source files, sections, chapters and examples were examined; what could not be retrieved; and whether the public interactive site was actually operated or only its HTML read. Reading a summary or chapter headings does not equal reading all chapters.

**First recognition exercise.** A door has closed; afterward it is closed. One person witnesses the event and another observes only the condition. Before naming any cell, say precisely what each can know and what would count as an adequate answer to each person's question.

**Second recognition exercise.** A sentence describing the closing is neither the physical movement nor the reader's understanding of that sentence. Keep three possible completion-events apart:

1. A **worldly occurrence**.
2. The **sentence's own linguistic movement and landing**.
3. The **listener's or reader's recognition**.

Never transfer a coordinate between these merely because the subject matter is shared.

**Third recognition exercise.** Consider a passage wide enough for a person but too narrow for a loaded basket. What is complete *for which undertaking*? If another route permits the basket to arrive, what still remains blocked? Be exact before employing words such as Opening, Repair, or Release.

## 2. How to investigate

The inquiry proceeds by a repeatable discipline:

**Encounter → distinguish the undertaking → recognize the demand → identify what would count as enough → test whether it occurred → determine what remains available → compare the nearest rival → state the limited result → earn the next question.**

The three governing questions remain:

1. **What changed?** Transformation Pattern: Differentiation, Opening, Relating, Repair, Integration, Settlement.
2. **What made it sufficient?** Completion Topology: Focal, Distributed, Release.
3. **How does the achievement remain available?** Persistence Mode: Standing, Bearing, Efficacy.

These are **analytically different questions** about **one achievement**. Their answers are not independent attributes that can simply be pasted together. The [controlling reference](../THE_54_CONTROL.md) requires *Dimensional Conjunction*: reciprocal determination within the same completion-event.

**Do not confuse:**

- A true statement with an adequate answer to a particular question.
- An event with the several intelligible achievements it may support.
- Completion of one undertaking with completion of every undertaking concerning its subject.
- A newly arising question with evidence that an earlier completion was defective.
- An illustration of a form with a demonstration that the form is necessary.
- The physical disappearance of an obstruction with **Release**, in which its *governing force* ceases.
- A corrected fault with mere improvement; **Repair** requires a governing misfit relative to an operative criterion.
- An agreeable ending with **Settlement**; settlement requires a live demand actually discharged.
- A persistence mode with any incidental later consequence. Its role must be **constitutive of that particular achievement**.

For every proposed coordinate, give the exact **undertaking and completion-event**, its conditions of sufficiency, and the strongest neighbouring coordinate. Ask which counterfactual change would invalidate the candidate form *as that achievement*. An interpretive description that survives the removal of its allegedly constitutive feature has not yet earned its conjunction.

## 3. The current argument ledger

**Status vocabulary**

- **Recognised distinction:** A difference that can be encountered and described; not yet a proof of a complete taxonomy.
- **Argued principle:** A stated conclusion supported by an explicit, scope-limited argument. Its assumptions must remain visible.
- **Structural proposal:** A definition or theory proposed for further demonstration, even if it has many compelling examples.
- **Research target:** A claim requiring a new derivation, counterfactual discrimination or prospective test.
- **Tested result:** A recorded experiment with disclosed conditions, original outputs, rivals and limits; agreement is not identical to proof.
- **Revised or defeated:** A formerly recorded claim whose scope or validity has changed, with a traceable reason.

A result may have a strong argument yet remain untested. Conversely, a test can reveal a pattern without proving a philosophical necessity. Do not force every contribution onto a single linear scale.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **IC-001** | **Truth does not automatically supply sufficiency for every undertaking.** A true report of a door's present state does not, by itself, answer what event brought it about. | **Argued principle** | The demand specifies what the answer must determine. Distinct questions may have distinct success conditions. |
| **IC-002** | **An expression can be corrected through a difference it does not yet express adequately.** | **Argued principle, bounded** | The cracked-vessel and translation cases show that recognition of inadequacy can precede an adequate formulation. This does not alone settle the complete relation between cognition, concepts and language. |
| **IC-003** | **Completion is demand-relative without being arbitrary.** | **Argued principle** | The load and passage may physically remain the same while adequacy differs by undertaking; material and practical constraints still determine whether passage succeeds. |
| **IC-004** | **A subsequent question does not automatically cancel a completed earlier undertaking.** | **Argued principle** | Derived below as demand-relative non-cancellation. The original achievement may be undermined if a condition of its sufficiency proves unsatisfied. |

The undertaking must individuate the achievement before a coordinate is assigned. |
| **IC-006** | **Transformation, topology and persistence jointly constitute a candidate completion identity.** | **Structural proposal** | The canonical rule of Dimensional Conjunction states this. Its necessity must be demonstrated through discriminating neighbouring forms, not restated. |
| **IC-007** | **Standing, Bearing and Efficacy are distinct *constitutive* ways of persisting.** | **Structural proposal** | A result may also exhibit incidental standing, dependencies and effects. Determine which makes it *this* completion and test by counterfactual removal. |
| **IC-008** | **Every eligible completion-event has one uniquely appropriate conjunctive identity among the 54.** | **Research target** | Individuation, discriminability and uniqueness require evidence. Several descriptions of one physical happening do not alone refute uniqueness; unstable assignment for a *precisely individuated* undertaking matters. |
| **IC-009** | **The 54 are exhaustive and irreducible.** | **Research target** | The calculation 6 × 3 × 3 indexes 54 positions; it does not demonstrate empirical or conceptual exhaustiveness. |

The language argument IC-002 does not deductively entail exactly 54 forms. No ledger entry may silently borrow the truth of a research target as a premise.

## 4. First derived principle: demand-relative non-cancellation

The first advance to record is intentionally elementary. It is **not** a derivation of all 54, but a condition that any adequate theory of completion must respect.

**Starting situation:** A mathematician is asked to establish that an equation has *at least one solution*. Call that undertaking **U₁**.

**Step A — Identify its demand.** A demonstration of a valid instance satisfying the equation under its original conditions is sufficient for the existential question. The proof need not answer every question about the equation.

**Step B — Recognise the achieved sufficiency.** Suppose such a valid instance is proved. U₁ has been completed.

**Step C — Introduce a further demand.** Someone asks whether the solution is **unique**, undertaking **U₂**. U₂ may be unanswered, but the absence of uniqueness proof does not defeat the existence proof.

**Step D — Compare an actual defeat.** Suppose, instead, that the supposed solution violates a domain restriction belonging to U₁. If that restriction was essential to the proof and no adequate witness remains established, the apparent completion of U₁ may be defeated.

**Result — IC-004:**

> A later undertaking does not render an earlier intelligible achievement incomplete merely by arising. It defeats that achievement only insofar as it shows a condition constitutive of the earlier undertaking's sufficiency was not, or is no longer, satisfied in the relevant respect.

**Cross-domain contrast.** A door may be made weatherproof while remaining impossible to open from inside. The second defect adds an unmet demand; it does not, by itself, undo weatherproofing. But water entering through the supposedly repaired seal under the original specified conditions challenges the weatherproofing achievement itself.

**Further implication — IC-005.** One physical event can support more than one achievement. Therefore **individuate the undertaken completion before demanding a unique coordinate**. “The door was repaired” is often too coarse; *which defect was overcome, under which success condition, with what achieved availability?* is the relevant question.

**Limit:** A comparable distinction is available in ordinary task reasoning and existing mathematics. Recording it here does not show that the three-dimensional grammar is needed to derive it. That further explanatory gain is precisely what the next inquiry must investigate.

## 5. The next earned test

**Current frontier question (Edition 1):**

> **Can Dimensional Conjunction identify a necessary condition, exclude a purported completion, or predict a relevant difference *before* an unfamiliar case's outcome is disclosed—and do so more precisely than analysis of the undertaking's success conditions alone?**

This is the proposed bridge from a powerful taxonomy to a possible logic of intelligible completion.

### Test A — Establish the undertaking before the coordinate

Supply fresh cases not selected to exemplify particular cells. Include cases of prevention, maintenance, mathematical proof, misleading translation, practical workarounds, distributed coordination and discovered impossibility.

For each case, conceal the outcome initially. The investigator records:

1. The **undertaking** and what would make it sufficient.
2. The **opening unresolvedness** and precisely what has not yet occurred.
3. Whether the presented material allows a **completion judgment at all**. If not: **no completion, no coordinate**.
4. A prediction of what would **defeat** completion, what would **leave it intact**, and what would merely open a separate undertaking.

Freeze this record before showing the outcome or the canonical coordinate.

### Test B — Test whether the conjunction adds something

In a distinct stage, propose the governing unified form using the primitive definitions—but do not assign three labels and then claim a conjunction. Specify a **counterfactual identity test**:

- If the focal locus ceased to determine sufficiency, would this still be the same achievement?
- If an indispensable distributed contribution failed, could this still count as complete?
- If the old constraint remained physically present but no longer governed the undertaking, what was released?
- If the claimed standing determination, constitutive bearing or operative efficacy were removed, would the **identity of the original completion** survive?

Use the questions appropriate to the candidate rather than all of them mechanically. Compare the **strongest neighbouring identity**, holding two dimensions fixed where informative. Explain exactly what each candidate predicts and why they diverge.

Then reveal the outcome. Preserve confirmation, disagreement, ambiguous interpretation and justified refusal alike.

### Test C — Compare a rival without the 54

Ask whether a careful account of **undertaking-relative success conditions alone** makes the same prediction. If yes, the result is not yet evidence of added explanatory power for Dimensional Conjunction. If no, identify the precise prediction or exclusion contributed by the unified structure.

**A valuable result may be failure.** A well-specified undertaking whose alleged constitutive persistence can be removed without changing the achievement would expose an inadequacy in the proposed conjunction. That would be a genuine advance, not an embarrassment to hide.

**Do not turn this proposal into a claim that tests have been run.** This edition records a test design, not a dataset, a successful blind trial, or an established derivation from all three dimensions.

## 6. How a visiting investigator contributes

A visiting GPT or human may contribute a better explanation, an objection, a new principle, a counterexample or a test. The objective is **one defensible additional step**, not compulsory agreement.

Before answering:

1. Supply a **reading audit**, including inaccessible files and whether the interactive website was used or only source inspected.
2. Reconstruct the relevant claim in its strongest accurate form, using the original manuscripts and [control](../THE_54_CONTROL.md). Do not substitute a more familiar theory for it.
3. Locate the **exact ledger IDs** affected.
4. State what is **already recognised or argued** and what is **newly inferred**.
5. Give the shortest explicit reasoning chain sufficient to support the new result.
6. Offer the **strongest nearby rival**, and say what observable, conceptual or counterfactual difference decides between them.
7. Report whether your result **depends on** the 54 or can be obtained without it.
8. Identify what the argument **does not establish**, and what precise question it earns next.

## 7. Keep philosophical inquiry distinct from blind tests

This guide, the manuscripts, the decks and the ledger are **informed, non-blind** materials. A GPT that has seen them cannot use the same conversation to claim a blind coordinate result.

The existing instruments remain independently authoritative and **unchanged**:

- [GPT-A — Blind Sentence Assay](GPT_A_BLIND_SENTENCE_ASSAY.md): use a **fresh isolated context**, without grammar, atlas, decks, intended coordinate or this guide. Apply **no completion, no coordinate**; freeze the complete internal-movement transfer verbatim.
- [GPT-B — Structural Decoder](GPT_B_STRUCTURAL_DECODER.md): receive the exact source sentence and frozen GPT-A transfer, plus its prescribed primitive-grammar prompt, **not** the canonical cell lookup. Test the strongest rival; freeze its result.
- [Persistence Coordinator and GPT-D round-trip](PERSISTENCE_COORDINATOR_SOURCE.md): administer comparisons only after subordinate outputs are frozen. GPT-D's fresh derivation must not see the original sentence or canonical table prematurely.

The [GitHub Action](../.github/workflows/a-voyage-into-sensemaking.yml) records a prompted voyage through an issue template. **It does not autonomously execute GPT-A or GPT-B or certify validity**. Do not attribute test results to it.

A philosophical argument and a successful automated classification are different achievements. Even strong agreement between decoders would not demonstrate the 54's necessity or exhaustiveness; a sound philosophical insight could also remain valid if automated classification proves unreliable.

## 8. How this living record advances

Edition 1 begins a **cumulative argument ledger**, not a replacement for the canonical forms or their original manuscripts. Future revisions should:

1. Preserve the original argument and its **stable claim ID**, revising its status or scope explicitly rather than silently rewriting intellectual history.
2. Distinguish **new evidence, new derivation, repeated illustration, and editorial interpretation**.
3. Cite the exact source, date, instrument version and original result for any claimed experiment.
4. Record **dependencies**: what a proposed conclusion needs, what it would establish, and which later claim it would license.
5. Use the **narrowest defensible conclusion** without deflating a genuine achievement into a vague request for more research.
6. Record consequential objections, contradictions, failed predictions and alternative explanations on equal terms with successful cases.
7. End each edition with **one frontier question whose answer would materially change the ledger**.
8. Never modify the canonical definitions or frozen experimental prompts as a side effect of updating this record. Changes to those require their own deliberate, versioned decisions.
