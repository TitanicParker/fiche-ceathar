# How Understanding Grows — A Working Vocabulary of Clarity

**Fiche Ceathar · 6 November 2026 · Philosophical reading companion**

**Purpose.** This glossary helps a reader distinguish the achievement of intelligible completion from the processes through which people encounter, express, retain, transmit, and build upon that achievement. Its terms are aids to recognition, not substitutes for examining actual cases. The canonical definitions and cell identities remain in [THE_54_CONTROL.md](../THE_54_CONTROL.md); this companion does not add dimensions or revise that reference.

**The central movement:** Something becomes sufficiently clear for an undertaking. That achievement can remain available, become a condition for further inquiry, be refined, or be corrected. Clarity is not the accumulation of words; it is increasingly precise recognition of the demand, the determination that answers it, what remains achieved, and what is now entitled to govern.

## I. The local achievement — what became enough?

| Term | Working meaning | Do not confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| **Intelligibility** | The determinacy through which something can be encountered, distinguished, related, recognized, or expressed adequately. | A particular word, utterance, person's recognition, or claim's truth. |
| **Understanding** | A person's achieved grasp of something intelligible, adequate in a specified respect. | The intelligible relation itself, or total knowledge of its subject. |
| **Demand / undertaking** | The particular question, need, task, or intelligible requirement to which an attempted achievement must answer. | Every possible future question concerning the same subject. |
| **Operative Centre** | The minimal governing determination by which the specified achievement becomes sufficient and remains achieved. It may be relational and distributed. | A grammatical head, a physically central location, or every contributing operation. |
| **Sufficiency** | The conditions under which the undertaking has actually been met. | Mere acceptance, the stopping of an exchange, or exhaustive knowledge. |
| **Resolution** | The achieved sufficiency of a particular demand, whether through a statement, practical action, relation, repair, or other completion. | The canonical **Settlement** Transformation Pattern, which is one kind of transformation. |
| **Transformation Pattern** | Which kind of unresolvedness is overcome: **Differentiation, Opening, Relating, Repair, Integration, Settlement**. | An arbitrary change of objects, words, or emotions. |
| **Completion Topology** | What *constitutes sufficiency* for the achievement: **Focal** (a decisive locus), **Distributed** (a connected extent or plurality), or **Release** (a prior governing condition loses its determining force). | Three merely spatial shapes. A Release may involve one physical locus or many. |
| **Persistence Mode** | How the achieved determination remains available *as achieved*: **Standing**, **Bearing**, or **Efficacy**. | The media of transmission, the duration of the original event, or what happens chronologically afterward. |
| **Dimensional Conjunction** | The reciprocal constitution of one completed achievement by Transformation Pattern, Completion Topology, and Persistence Mode. Neither dimension is simply a label appended to a completed event. | Three independent tags, a Cartesian multiplication that proves itself, or an extra fourth dimension. |

**The generative reading.** Ask of one properly individuated achievement: *What unresolvedness changed? What made that change enough? How does the result remain achieved?* Compare the answers together, testing how each constrains the others. A cell name is a useful compression handle; its wording is not an additional primitive condition beyond its constitutive identity.

**A sentence is not automatically the unit.** One sentence may contain several operations, and one episode may contain several completed achievements. Specify the undertaking and scale first; distinguish the **under-field** contributing to a particular landing from the **landing** itself and from the larger field that later inherits it.

## II. The continuing achievement — what becomes available afterward?

| Term | Working meaning | Do not confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| **Moment** | The occasion of an intelligible achievement or recognition, which may unfold over time rather than occupy an instantaneous clock-point. | The achieved determination's entire lifetime. |
| **Continuation** | A further undertaking becomes possible or relevant after a particular achievement has been completed. | Proof that the earlier achievement was incomplete. |
| **Remainder** | A general word for what is left in view after a local completion; **always specify the kind**: a surviving result, an unresolved demand, a newly available possibility, or a question not yet articulated. | One undifferentiated leftover or a fourth persistence mode. |
| **Inheritance** | An identifiable dependency by which an earlier achievement constrains what a later undertaking can legitimately establish or use. | Mere repetition, resemblance, or later chronological occurrence. |
| **Transmission** | The passing or re-expression of what was achieved through words, diagrams, memories, records, practices, or other carriers. | The persistence mode of the achievement itself. |
| **Reconstruction** | Recovering a route or justification through which a result can be examined or established again. | Merely possessing or quoting the result. |
| **Result-survivor** | What remains established or available as a result. | The original act or the route by which the result was established. |
| **Path-survivor** | What remains available of the route, proof, reasoning, or practice through which a result can be checked or reconstructed. | The bare statement of a conclusion. |
| **Traversal** | A dependency-ordered development in which prior achievements change what later achievements can legitimately establish. | A sequence of statements merely occurring one after another. |

A theorem's statement may remain while its proof is lost. A proof may be reconstructed by someone absent from the original discovery. A valid earlier result may be inapplicable to a new problem. **Persistence, transmission, reconstructibility, and entitlement to further use answer different questions.**

## III. The movement towards clarity — what must be preserved or changed?

| Term | Working meaning | Do not confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| **Correction** | Revising an earlier judgment where an essential claim or condition was mistaken. Preserve what was actually established. | Every subsequent change of circumstance. |
| **Supersession** | A later condition or achievement takes the place of an earlier one for a present undertaking without necessarily making the earlier achievement false. | Proof of an earlier error. |
| **Release** | As a *Completion Topology*, sufficiency through a formerly governing condition ceasing to determine the result. | Repair, simple removal, isolation, or unqualified freedom. |
| **Governing authority** | In structural inquiry, the standing of a condition to decide what counts as sufficient for this specified undertaking. | Social permission, legal power, political rule, or the claim that a past condition was never valid. |
| **Legitimate re-entry** | The use of an earlier achievement in a new undertaking while the conditions relevant to that use remain satisfied or are re-established. | Blindly repeating a conclusion or assuming that any past success governs every new setting. |
| **Clarity** | Increasingly exact recognition of the undertaking, its sufficient determination, what remains established, and what no longer properly governs the current judgment. | Confidence, information volume, final certainty, or the disappearance of every further question. |

**A correction is not a rewind.** If a meeting was always on Thursday but was announced as Tuesday, the announcement needs correction. If it really was on Tuesday and was then moved to Thursday, the original statement need not have been false. The current answer is the same word—Thursday—but the intelligible histories and their governing conditions differ.

**Release is not a spatial oddity.** Focal, Distributed, and Release are comparable through their role in sufficiency. A condition once necessary may cease to govern while its earlier legitimacy and intelligibility remain intact.

## IV. Four guiding distinctions

1. **What is intelligible is not identical with its expression or someone's understanding of it.** People can encounter the same relation through different words, moments, and paths of recognition.
2. **A resolved undertaking is not the end of inquiry.** A sufficient answer can stand while a newly available question becomes the next undertaking.
3. **What remains achieved is not identical with how it is transmitted or justified.** Distinguish a surviving result from its recoverable path.
4. **What remains intelligible is not necessarily what still governs.** Changed conditions can alter an earlier achievement's authority over the present without erasing what was validly achieved then.

## V. A compact reading test

A garden boundary was made to hold soil. Yesterday the gardeners observed that soil had passed beneath some stones. Today they learn how water carries it; tomorrow they may change the slope. Their earlier observation survives, the investigation continues, and a later explanation becomes available. If a different boundary now works, the old requirement to fix each stone may cease to govern, though the original boundary and the historical reason for making it remain intelligible.

Do not assign one coordinate to the whole three-day story. Ask what *one* undertaking was completed at *one* indicated scale, and what that completion leaves available for the next. The larger narrative is a traversal of achievements, not automatically a single 54-cell achievement.

Identify a specific weakness plainly when one exists. Do not substitute serial general disclaimers for philosophical investigation.

**The task is to move towards clarity, one sufficiently achieved understanding at a time.**
