The Pythagorean Gate has a history

The provenance narrative places a geometrical distinction at the beginning. In an orthogonal basis, the cross-term of a quadratic metric vanishes. In a 60-degree basis it is present. This difference can be stated precisely, without converting it into a proof of every philosophical proposition later explored.

The cosine rule

a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos(A)

A unit 60° basis

Q(m,n) = m² + n² + mn

At a right angle, the cosine term is zero. At 60 degrees, it contributes a fixed coupling between coordinate directions. This mathematical distinction was understood in the earlier work as an invitation to examine the part played by relation in form.

Not the same as the current method

In the earlier sentence writing, State / Relation / Event and Toward / Across / Away are organising terms. In the current canon, the three axes are Transformation Pattern, Completion Topology and Persistence Mode, simultaneously determining one cell. They must not be mechanically equated.

What history changes

It shows that the philosophical and geometrical horizons were already present before the later sentence-reading instrument. The Metaphysical Corner therefore contains both questions opened by today's work and older questions that helped bring the work into being.
